## Rules fixed before the ranks

- Window: 2013–2023. Rank 1 is the highest value. Ties take the minimum rank.
- The median and interquartile range include Germany and every other country with a non-missing value that year.
- A share “fell” or “rose” only if the 2013–2023 change exceeds 2 percentage points. Otherwise it stayed within that band.
- Retailer density “fell” or “rose” only if the 2023 level is at least 10% away from 2013.
- Density ranks use country-years with final gas consumption of at least 1 TWh.
- RQ3 enters the abstract only if every leave-one-out coefficient on retailer density has the same sign as the baseline and lies within 50% of it. The baseline is two-way fixed effects of retailers per TWh on the cumulative generation share, with standard errors clustered by country.

## RQ2. Gas retailers per TWh

- Germany’s retailer density rose: 1.28 retailers per TWh in 2013 and 1.76 in 2023. The median moved from 0.77 to 1.15. Rank 8 of 28 in 2013 and 11 of 30 in 2023 (1 = most retailers per TWh).
- Robustness, retailers per million inhabitants on the same 1 TWh screen: Germany 10.25 in 2013 (rank 2 of 28) and 11.71 in 2023 (rank 2 of 29). Per person, Germany stays near the top. Per terawatt-hour, it does not. The headline rank is the per-terawatt-hour rank.
- The top of the 2023 density ranking is small markets just above the 1 TWh floor: EE (21 retailers, 1.9 TWh); MD (24 retailers, 3.7 TWh); BA (7 retailers, 1.6 TWh). Density removes the raw-count advantage of a large market, and it is noisy when the market is only a few terawatt-hours.
- On the unscaled 2023 count, Germany ranks 1 of 33 with 973 retailers. That rank is market size. The density rank above is the one the contract uses.

## RQ3. Exploratory association

- Baseline: a one-percentage-point higher cumulative generation share is associated with 0.003 retailers per TWh (cluster-robust 95% interval -0.017 to 0.022; within R² 0.001; 30 country clusters).
- Same sample, unscaled retailer count as the outcome: coefficient -0.025 (interval -0.484 to 0.433). Country fixed effects remove permanent differences in market size, so this regression is not where the size confound shows up. The 2023 ranks are.
- Leave-one-out coefficients range from -0.005 to 0.008. 29 of 30 keep the baseline sign, and 25 of 30 stay within 50% of it. The baseline interval includes zero and the within R² is about zero.
- Abstract rule: the density coefficient does not keep both its sign and its rough magnitude across every leave-one-out. RQ3 stays out of the abstract.
